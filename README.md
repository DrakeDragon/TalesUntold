# Tales Untold

*once upon a time…*

A serene, atmospheric, endless fairytale slider for phones and browsers. You glide a storybook hero down an endless, hand-painted hillside through **30 fairytale realms**, chased by a wolf, leaping gaps, grinding ribbon rails, riding loops and walls, diving through dunes and slingshotting back up, fighting off realm bosses, and collecting coins to upgrade a workshop and dress your hero.

It is a single self-contained HTML file. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls, no ads, no tracking. Progress is saved on the device.

---

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox), on a phone or a computer.
2. Tap **Begin the Journey**.

**Hosting on GitHub Pages:** put `index.html` (and the empty `.nojekyll` file) at the root of a repository, then Settings → Pages → deploy from the branch. The page will be live at `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/`.

On iPhone, Safari's "Add to Home Screen" launches it without browser chrome. Landscape feels best; portrait also works.

## How to play

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Jump | **Tap** (keyboard: Space, Up Arrow or W) |
| Flip for points | **Hold** while in the air, then land smooth |
| Dive | **Hold** while sliding downhill; release on the climb to slingshot |
| Pause | Pause button (keyboard: P or Esc) |
| Grind a rail | Jump onto a ribbon rail and ride it |
| Loops and wall rides | Hit them at speed and stay on; they run themselves |

**Core loop.** You slide automatically and the speed builds over distance. Jump obstacles, clear gaps, land smoothly (matching the slope) for *perfect landing* bonuses, chain tricks into a **combo multiplier**, and collect coins. Fleeing **storybook critters** can be caught for bonus points.

**Reading the world.** Hazards are drawn with a distinct dark outline and a warning mark so they never blend into scenery. Obstacle groups keep a minimum travel distance apart so a single slip does not cascade.

**Hearts and the spark meter.** You start each run with one heart (more with the Hearthstone upgrade). Perfect landings, coins and bounces fill a **spark meter**; filling it earns another heart (maximum five). Heart pickups also appear in the world. A hit with a heart in hand costs a heart instead of ending the run, so you can keep playing as long as you keep playing well.

**Turn the Page.** After a fall you can spend coins to revive. The price rises each time in a run and is reduced by the Fairy Godmother upgrade.

**The chaser.** A wolf (a different named chaser in every realm) starts pursuing you. Outrun it for bonuses; stumbles let it catch up.

**Bounce and height.** Perfect landings can bounce you back up; height and distance both matter for score. Some realms make the ground springy.

### Power-ups

Fairy Wings (fly), Wisp Lantern (light the dark), Seven-League Boots (smash hazards), Magic Mirror (a shield), Golden Goose (coins worth double), plus Heart pickups.

### Set pieces and bosses

- **Falling Stars** and **Gold Rush** set pieces fill the sky or ground with coins.
- Halfway through every realm a **realm boss** appears (150 distinct bosses: giants, dragons, a djinn, serpents, queens, beasts, a walking hut and more). Dodge their attacks to outlast them. Take no hits for a **Flawless** bonus.

## Progression

- **Quests and rank.** Each rank presents a set of quests (distance, coins, flips, grinds, critters, combos, perfect landings, wolf escapes, realms visited, airtime). Complete them all to **rank up**.
- **Heroes (40).** New heroes unlock as your rank rises (rank 1 to 78), each with a perk: Little Red, Gretel, Jack, Scheherazade, Briar Rose, Gerda, Cinderella, Little Mermaid, The Piper, Rapunzel, The Frog Prince, The Nutcracker, The Birch Crone, Thumbelina, The Nightingale, The Snow Queen.
- **Workshop (22 upgrades).** Hearthstone, Fairy Wings, Wisp Lantern, Seven-League Boots, Golden Goose, Magic Mirror, Coin Charm, Golden Purse, Padded Cloak, Showman's Flair, Fairy Luck, Fairy Godmother, Wolf Ward. Bought with coins; each has multiple levels.
- **Wardrobe.** Sleds: Silver Spoon, Gingerbread Sleigh, Pumpkin Coach, Glass Slipper, Magic Carpet, Swan Boat, Crescent Moon, Dragon Scale. Trails: Stardust, Rose Petals, Bubbles, Embers, Piper Notes, Hearts, Rainbow Ribbon, Comet Tail. Each has a live preview.
- **Trophies (71).** Distance, tricks, bosses, loops and walls, realms, wardrobe, festivals, daily streaks, sharing and more, each with a coin reward.
- **Realms book.** Jump back to start from any realm you have reached.

## Game modes and extras

- **Daily Tale.** One shared course per day with a twist (Gold Rush, Moonlit Ride, Hazard Season, Fairy Fortune, or Hurry, Hurry!). Everyone gets the same course for the day; build a streak and earn bonuses.
- **Festivals (time-limited events).** A themed festival is always running: Witching Hour (Oct 15 – Nov 2), Harvest Moon Fair (Nov 3 – 30), Snow Queen's Frost Fair (Dec 1 – Jan 6), Spring Bloom Fete (Mar 15 – Apr 20) and Midsummer Night's Revel (Jun 15 – Jul 15). Outside those dates a weekly event rotates (Dragon, Mermaid, Giants, Fairy Week). Gather the event's glowing star tokens during runs (**double on weekends**) to pass reward tiers worth coins; seasonal festivals also award an **exclusive trail and sled** that you keep forever. Event items are earned only, never sold.
- **Zen Ride.** No wolf, no pressure; just the scenery.
- **Photo mode.** Pause, change the time of day, and save a picture.
- **Share card.** A designed results card (distance, score, realm, hero, tier medal) you can share or save.
- **Save export / import.** Copy a save code to move progress between devices.
- **Settings.** Landing assist, weather, time of day, effects quality (Low/Medium/High), HUD, screen shake, zoom, sound, music and vibration.
- **Audio.** Procedural music (a different scale and key per realm) and sound effects, generated live with the Web Audio API; there are no audio files.

## Technical notes

- One file: HTML + CSS + JavaScript, rendered on a 2D canvas. About 230 KB.
- Terrain is generated procedurally as you ride; the course planner spaces hazards and features by travel time so difficulty stays fair as speed rises.
- The renderer lowers its resolution automatically if the frame rate drops on slower phones; **Settings → Effects** can also reduce particles.
- Saves live in the browser's `localStorage` under the key `onceUponASlope` (an older working-title key, kept so existing saves keep working). Clearing site data erases progress, so use **Export** to back up.
- Works offline once loaded. No external requests.

## Testing and known limitations

Automated tests run against a headless Chrome build: a 12-run bot test at 150 s each, per-realm bot runs for every realm, boss encounter bots, a 25-minute continuous run through 25 realms, a full UI flow test (shop, wardrobe, save export/import, share card), festival and date-logic tests, and visual checks of every realm and screen at phone sizes. All passed with no console errors.

Honest gaps:

- **Not yet tested on physical phones.** Touch feel, real frame rates, and notch/safe-area layout are verified only in emulation.
- **Audio has not been listened to by the developer.** It was built and checked for errors, not auditioned.
- A small residual chance of an unlucky death exists in very long runs, especially when playing sloppily.
- Very short landscape screens (around 320 px tall) can scroll the title footer slightly.
- Saved progress from early builds that had only 16 realms will place Tale II earlier than before; nothing is lost.

Something (or someone) called the Unwriter is tearing out the last pages. Each realm's boss holds a page; beating it restores it.

The story is told without ever pausing play:
- When you reach a realm for the first time, a short story passage fades in under the realm name (about 9 seconds, top of the screen).
- Defeating a boss shows 'Page restored'.
- The whole story is collected in **Realms > Read the Story**, split into five acts: The Torn Page, The Apprentice, The First Storyteller, The Margins and The Unwriting.
- (Moved to realm 150, see the final update.) Beating the boss of the final realm plays the finale and unlocks the trophy The Final Line. The book then starts again as Tale II.
